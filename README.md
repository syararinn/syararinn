About me

I’m a non-engineer developing software with AI-assisted coding tools.

In less than a year, I’ve built around 10 software projects. My projects are mainly inspired by real-world problems and workflows I encounter in areas such as drones, photography, mapping, information monitoring, and work automation.

I use AI not only to generate code, but also to explore ideas, design systems, test approaches, review implementations, and continuously improve the software.

Most of my projects are currently private because they are still under development or related to specialized professional workflows.

How I Develop

I do not have a traditional software engineering background.

Instead, I combine domain knowledge, problem discovery, experimentation, and AI-assisted development to turn ideas from real-world workflows into working software.

My development process typically involves:

Problem → Research → Design → AI-assisted implementation → Testing → Review → Improvement

I’m particularly interested in how people without traditional programming backgrounds can use AI to build useful software based on knowledge and experience from their own fields.
